## About Me
I am a geophysicist and researcher interested in applying
geophysical imaging, numerical modelling and artificial
intelligence to subsurface and environmental problems.
My research interests include:
- Ground-Penetrating Radar (GPR)
- Environmental Geophysics

- Deep Learning

## Research
My research explores the combination of traditional
geophysical methods with modern computational and
machine-learning techniques.
Current research directions include:
### Ground-Penetrating Radar
Development and application of GPR methods for
subsurface and environmental investigations.
### Physics-Informed Deep Learning
Using physical knowledge and scientific constraints
to improve machine-learning approaches for geophysical
imaging and interpretation.
### Frozen Lakes and Environmental Geophysics
Exploring GPR applications in frozen environments,
including ice and methane-related subsurface studies.
