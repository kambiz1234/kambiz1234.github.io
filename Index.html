ABOUT ME

Science beneath the surface.

I am a geophysicist and researcher interested in understanding Earth's subsurface and environmental systems through geophysical imaging, numerical modelling and artificial intelligence.

My research interests include Ground-Penetrating Radar (GPR), environmental geophysics, frozen environments, subsurface contamination and physics-informed machine learning.

RESEARCH

Research interests

Ground-Penetrating Radar

GPR imaging, interpretation and numerical approaches for investigating subsurface and environmental systems.

Environmental Geophysics

Applying geophysical methods to environmental challenges, frozen environments and subsurface characterization.

Artificial Intelligence

Exploring machine learning and deep learning methods for geophysical data analysis and interpretation.

Physics-Informed Machine Learning

Combining physical laws and scientific knowledge with modern machine-learning approaches.

PROJECTS

Selected research directions

Physics-Informed Deep Learning for GPR

Research into physics-informed deep learning approaches for subsurface contamination detection using Ground-Penetrating Radar imaging.

GPR and Frozen Lakes

Investigating applications of Ground-Penetrating Radar for frozen lakes, ice thickness, freezing processes and methane-related subsurface signals.

Numerical Geophysical Modelling

Computational modelling of geophysical systems and environmental processes.
